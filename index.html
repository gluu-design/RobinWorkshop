From France to New York

At age 16, Robin Miellet began his apprenticeship with the Compagnons du Devoir, France's most prestigious craftsmanship guild. This demanding eight-year journey took Robin across various French regions, where he mastered techniques passed down from the era of the great cathedrals.

From 2007 to 2015, he perfected the design and fabrication of metal art pieces, a period that culminated in the creation of his masterpiece. In 2016, Robin joined La Forge De Style in New Jersey; today, he brings this heritage of excellence to New York, creating unique bronze art and bespoke furniture.
