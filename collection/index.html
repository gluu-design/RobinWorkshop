C-Light Collection

Sculptural Floor Lamps

The C-Light Collection is a series of sculptural floor lamps designed for large living spaces. With its distinctive curved "C" shape, the lamp brings light from a distance, extending elegantly over seating areas and coffee tables.

The Story

The C-Light Collection is a new Atelier Banger lighting series designed for expansive interiors and large living rooms.

Defined by its iconic "C" shape, the lamp is conceived to start near a wall or architectural element and extend outward, bringing light precisely above a coffee table or central living area — without the need for ceiling fixtures.

Each lamp is engineered with a solid metal base that provides exceptional stability, allowing for a long cantilevered reach. The upper structure is fabricated in aluminum to reduce overall weight while preserving strength and balance.

The C-Light is available in three sizes — Small, Medium, and Big — allowing the piece to adapt to different room scales and spatial compositions.

Three head geometries can be selected: round, oval, or square with softened edges, offering multiple design expressions while maintaining a cohesive aesthetic.

Finishes include natural metal tones, black patina, or fully custom powder-coated colors based on the client's preferences.

While the design carries the signature Atelier Banger identity, each lamp can be customized to meet the specific aesthetic and spatial needs of its owner.

The C-Light Collection is available by pre-order. For pricing and customization inquiries, clients are invited to contact Atelier Banger directly.

Arch Table

Steel, Bronze & Oak Dining Table

A dining table designed for a garden conservatory, combining steel structure, bronze connections, and a solid oak top with a subtle resin inlay.

The Story

Designed and fabricated for a garden conservatory, the Arch Table serves as both a functional dining surface and a structural object. The base is built entirely in steel — angled sections and cut plates assembled into a series of arched supports, with each element clearly expressed and purposefully placed.

All connections are mechanically assembled using visible fasteners and bronze pieces, reinforcing the table's technical and architectural character. The result is a deliberate contrast: robust steel structure and precise assembly, softened by the warmth of the materials and the rhythm of the arches.

The solid oak top features a restrained black resin inlay through the center — a sober take on the river table. Designed for conservatories, winter gardens, and transitional spaces, it reflects Atelier Banger's approach: durable construction, honest materials, refined detailing.

Beam Table

Steel & Brass Dining Table

The Beam Table is an Atelier Banger creation inspired by historic structural beams used in early American architecture. A dining table exploring the contrast between strength and refinement.

The Story

The Beam Table is an original Atelier Banger creation, drawing inspiration from traditional structural beams once widely used in large-scale architecture — notably during the era of landmark constructions such as the Brooklyn Bridge. The design reinterprets the language of heavy construction into a refined piece of furniture.

Two massive steel legs are shaped from a single beam-inspired form, carefully cut and sculpted to create a strong yet balanced foundation. These elements are connected by a central cross beam, which passes through the legs and becomes a defining structural feature of the table.

The connection is secured using small brass keys, intentionally visible, creating a subtle contrast between the dark metal structure and the warmth of the brass. This detail expresses the core idea behind the piece: a dialogue between robustness and delicacy — where a visually heavy structure is assembled through precise, almost fragile-looking connections.

Topped with a solid wood surface, the Beam Table stands as both a functional dining table and an architectural object. This piece is part of the Atelier Banger collection and is available by pre-order.

Facet Ribbon Chair

Faceted Aluminum & Leather Chair

The Facet Ribbon Chair is an original Atelier Banger creation exploring the contrast between strength and apparent lightness. Faceted aluminum panels are assembled using leather ribbons, creating the illusion that the chair is held together solely by delicate lacing.

The Story

The Facet Ribbon Chair is a signature piece designed and fabricated by Atelier Banger, developed through an extensive design research process — from early design exploration to material selection and final fabrication.

The chair is composed of faceted aluminum panels connected by leather ribbons, intentionally left visible. This construction creates a visual paradox: rigid metal surfaces appear to be held together only by flexible leather elements.

This detail gives the piece a unique sense of lightness and tension, inviting the viewer to question the structure itself — how can solid metal forms remain assembled through something so seemingly fragile?

Entirely fabricated in aluminum with a bright polished finish, the surface reflects light with depth and subtlety, without reaching a full mirror polish.

Conceived as both a functional object and a sculptural piece, the Facet Ribbon Chair embodies Atelier Banger's approach to furniture — where craftsmanship, geometry, and material contrast meet. This chair is part of the Atelier Banger permanent collection and remains available for purchase.

Facet TV Stand

Sculptural Folded Aluminum TV Stand

This custom aluminum TV stand is an original Atelier Banger creation, designed and fabricated as a sculptural furniture piece. Entirely made of folded aluminum, its sharp faceted geometry gives the object a strong architectural presence while maintaining visual balance and lightness.

The Story

This TV stand is a signature piece designed and fabricated by Atelier Banger. Conceived as a functional sculpture, the design explores faceted geometry, sharp angles, and precise folds to transform aluminum into a bold architectural object.

Entirely fabricated in aluminum with a polished bright finish, the piece reflects light subtly without reaching a mirror effect, enhancing its presence while preserving material depth.

Designed as an independent creation rather than a client commission, this TV stand represents Atelier Banger's approach to furniture: combining metal craftsmanship, design research, and sculptural expression into a single object.

Available for purchase through the Atelier Banger collection.
