About Atelier Banger

From the ancient traditions of French craftsmanship to the contemporary artistry of New York, a journey of dedication, mastery, and innovation.

Our Journey

2006

Apprenticeship Begins

Compagnons du Devoir

At age 16, Robin Miellet began his apprenticeship at France's most prestigious craftsmanship school, learning metalworking and obtaining his CAP diploma.

2013

Tour de France

Compagnons du Devoir

His eight-year journey across French regions, perfecting skills in locksmithing, metalworking, blacksmithing, and art history. Created educational video on "Rolling Mills" for apprentices.

387 h

2013

Masterpiece

Graduation Project

Robin’s graduation was the culmination of 387 hours of meticulous craftsmanship. His masterpiece—an Eiffel Tower-inspired steel table base—features intricate, oversized dovetail joinery that showcases his technical mastery.

2014

Teaching & Mentorship

Compagnons du Devoir

He managed students and retraining candidates, including a professional reintegration program for Michelin factory employees, and produced a promotional video about the metalworking trade.

2016

La Forge De Style

New Jersey

A versatile designer, fabricator, and project manager, he specializes in high-end bronze artistry, furniture, and architectural projects. His expertise covers every stage of production—including prototyping, patinas, and engravings—alongside managing the brand's visual communication.

2024

Atelier Banger

New York City

Robin Miellet founded his independent New York atelier to provide bespoke commissions, expert restoration, and collaborative design for the architectural and artistic communities, bridging French heritage with modern American design.
