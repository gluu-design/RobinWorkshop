The Process

Every commission follows a thoughtful, collaborative process to ensure exceptional results

1

Initial Consultation

We discuss your vision, requirements, and budget. This can be via phone, video call, or in-person at the atelier.

2

Concept & Design

I create sketches, technical drawings, and 3D renderings for your review and approval. Multiple iterations until perfect.

3

Material Selection

Choose from bronze types, steel grades, finishes, and patinas. I provide samples and guidance on durability and aesthetics.

4

Fabrication

Using traditional techniques and modern tools, I handcraft your piece. Regular progress updates and photos throughout.

5

Installation & Delivery

Professional installation or white-glove delivery. Final adjustments and care instructions provided.
